## 🎨 About me

- 🧱 **Pixel artist** — I build worlds one 32×32 tile at a time, with a small palette and a lot of love
- 🗺️ **CubeAsset** — platformer tileset packs with parallax: [Canyon Desert](https://cubeasset.itch.io/canyon-desert-platformer) · [Deep Cave](https://cubeasset.itch.io/deep-cave-platformer) · [Deep Forest](https://cubeasset.itch.io/deep-forest-platformer) · [Fantasy Field](https://cubeasset.itch.io/fantasy-field-platformer)
- 🚀 **CubeBig Studio** — [**Proxima**](https://cubebig.itch.io/proxima), a bullet-hell roguelike set in deep space, made with **Godot** (in development)
- 🎓 Teknik Informatika student at Politeknik Negeri Manado, waiting for graduation
- 🐧 Daily driver: CachyOS (Arch-based Linux)
- ✉️ Open for **commissions & collabs** — tilesets, game jam art, or a whole biome: [contact@rokifran.me](mailto:contact@rokifran.me)
